Dr. Gopendra Singh Dixit

Paediatric Orthopedic Surgeon

Dr. Gopendra Singh Dixit is a pioneer of paediatric orthopedics in Chhattisgarh. Dr Dixit Bilaspur Hospital is the state's first and still its only dedicated paediatric orthopedic hospital. With over two decades of experience in orthopedic and paediatric orthopedic surgery, he has restored mobility and improved quality of life for thousands of patients across Bilaspur and the surrounding region.

He specialises in early diagnosis and both non-surgical (Ponseti method) and surgical correction of complex paediatric bone and joint conditions, helping children walk, run and grow without limitation.

Qualifications

  • MBBS — Pt. JNM Medical College, Raipur
  • D'Orthopedics — NSCB Medical College, Jabalpur
  • IOA ST Fellowship in Paediatric Orthopedics — CMC Vellore
  • Paediatric Orthopedic Trauma Fellowship — Ortho Research Group & Ortho TV Global Academy

Areas of expertise

  • Paediatric bone fracture clinic — specialised treatment of fractures in children
  • Clubfoot (CTEV) clinic — Ponseti method and surgical management for infants and children
  • Flatfoot clinic — evaluation and treatment of flexible and rigid flatfoot in children and adults
  • Deformity correction and limb reconstruction — uneven limb lengths, bow legs, knock knees and complex bone deformities, using advanced frames and guided growth techniques
  • Cerebral palsy clinic — management of gait abnormalities, contractures and mobility limitations
  • DDH clinic — early screening, bracing and surgical correction of developmental hip dysplasia
  • Adult orthopedics — fracture management, joint pain, sports injuries and trauma care
  • Growth-related disorders — bone deformity correction in children and adolescents

Why families choose Dr. Dixit

  • Specialised fellowship training in paediatric orthopedic conditions
  • Focus on early intervention to prevent long-term disability
  • A personalised treatment plan for every child
  • Both conservative and surgical expertise under one roof

Dr. Akanksha Dixit

Obstetrician & Gynecologist, Infertility Specialist

Dr. Akanksha Dixit provides comprehensive, compassionate care for women at every stage of life — from adolescence through pregnancy and beyond. She is an infertility specialist, and treatment here is offered at a genuinely patient-friendly cost. Dr Dixit Bilaspur Hospital is a government-certified Level 1 infertility treatment centre.

Her practice focuses on safe deliveries, careful management of high-risk pregnancies, and accessible fertility guidance for couples beginning their family-planning journey. She has wide experience in treating gynecological disorders, menstrual problems and uterine conditions.

Qualifications

  • MBBS — Indira Gandhi Government Medical College, Nagpur
  • MS Obstetrics & Gynecology — LTMG Medical College & Hospital, Sion, Mumbai

Areas of expertise

  • High-risk pregnancy management — close monitoring and specialised care for complicated pregnancies
  • Normal and assisted delivery — safe, supportive labour and delivery services
  • Infertility treatment (primary level) — evaluation, counselling and first-line fertility treatment for couples

  • Antenatal and postnatal care — routine check-ups, monitoring and recovery support
  • Menstrual disorders and adolescent gynecology
  • Well-woman health check-ups

Why women choose Dr. Akanksha Dixit

Dr. Chandrakant Garg

Senior Physiotherapist — BPT, MIAP, CMT

Dr. Chandrakant Garg is a senior physiotherapist with 14 years of clinical experience, specially trained in paediatric rehabilitation and spinal physiotherapy. He works closely with Dr. Gopendra Singh Dixit to provide structured, goal-oriented rehabilitation for children with cerebral palsy, brachial plexus injury, flexible flat foot, congenital torticollis and other developmental conditions.

He also provides evidence-based physiotherapy for adults with spinal and joint disorders. His treatment plans combine manual therapy, targeted exercise protocols and modern electrotherapy equipment to help every patient regain strength, mobility and independence.

Qualifications

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY empanelled

Dr Dixit Bilaspur Hospital is proud to be empanelled under the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana, so eligible families receive quality orthopedic and gynecological treatment on a cashless basis, as per scheme guidelines.

Our team walks patients through the whole process — from eligibility verification to treatment approval — so advanced healthcare stays affordable. Bring your Ayushman card and a photo ID to the reception desk and we will check your eligibility while you wait.
